04 Verse Sitrep · 2956.09

State of the 'Verse

What is live right now, what our operations are built on, and what is coming. Verified 22 Sep 2026.

  1. LIVE 16 SEP
    Alpha 4.10.1 · Orison Relief Support

    Crusader pays to rebuild Orison after the Siege: hauling, medical runs, crafting and Nine Tails clearance under one progression track. Permanent SecondWind gear. Runs until 4.10.2 (~7 Oct).

  2. LIVE 3 SYS
    Stanton · Pyro · Nyx

    Three connected systems under server meshing. Nyx brought Levski and the QV Breaker stations. Castra is confirmed as system four (Alpha 5.0), no window yet.

  3. OPS 4.10
    Siege of Orison V2 · Instancing

    First on-demand instanced operation: closed instances for up to four operatives. Exactly the format our briefings are written for.

  4. OPS 4.8+
    Tactical Strike Groups · Nyx

    InterSec fleet raids on defended QV stations, built for seven-plus ships from capital down to boots on the ground, with carrier repair and rearm. Requires InterSec Rank III.

  5. OPS 4.7+
    Crafting online

    Item Fabricator and blueprints since March 2026. Mining, salvage and refining now feed the armory loop we play on the strategic layer.

  6. EVENT 9–28 SEP
    Pirate Week 2026

    Pirate Swarm is back, pirate Gladius and Caterpillar unlockable, Most Wanted contest closes 28 Sep 20:00 UTC.

  7. NEXT Q4 26
    Alpha 4.11 · Nyx I

    First Nyx planet, Genesis planet tech V5, Starchitect points of interest, heavy combat armor. 4.10.2 with a new event lands first.

  8. NEXT Q2 27
    Squadron 42

    Content complete, over 40 hours. Moved from IAE 2026 to Q2 2027 (Chairman's letter, 27 Aug 2026) to clear the GTA VI window. Backer play session 9–10 Oct. No traditional CitizenCon this October.
